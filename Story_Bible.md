# Story Bible: Aethelgard & The Shattered Bastion

> *"The Colossi did not die; they were driven into the deep rock. Now the crust fractures, and their blood—Prismatic Aether—poisons the springs."*

---

## 1. World Cosmology & Premise

### Aethelgard & The Old Covenant
Long ago, primordial god-sized entities known as **Titans** walked the world of Aethelgard. Through an ancient alliance known as the **Old Covenant**, mortal races wove divine monoliths called **Anchors** to bind the Titans deep within the subterranean crust of the continent.

For millennia, peace reigned. But now, the Anchors are cracking. Seeping up through the fractured stone comes **Prismatic Aether**—the volatile, glowing blood of the slumbering Colossi. Where Aether touches the surface:
- Wildlife mutates into crystalline horrors.
- Ancient royal catacombs awaken with relentless dead.
- Volcanic drakes and void aberrations emerge from the mantle.
- Rogue warbands and cults wage war for raw Prismatic Shards.

### The Protagonist: The Wayfarer
The player awakens as an unpledged **Wayfarer**, washed ashore with broken memory at the steps of **Haven’s Rest**—the last fortified citadel standing between civil society and the decaying wastes. As a Wayfarer unbound by old factions, you are chosen to venture outward, cleanse corrupted sectors, destroy the Anchor Guardians, and prevent the resurrection of the Prime Titan: **Gorgoroth, the Earth-Breaker**.

---

## 2. Central Hub: Haven's Rest

Haven's Rest serves as the final bastion of humanity and the player's primary operational sanctuary:

1. **The Iron Anvil**
   - **Torvald the Smith**: Master blacksmith who tempers base gear tiers.
   - **Prismatic Enchanter**: Arcane artisan who channels Prismatic Shards to reroll magical affixes (Prefixes & Suffixes) on weapons and armor.

2. **The Rusty Goblet**
   - **The Tavern & Inn**: Rest in feather beds to cleanse *Exhaustion*, restore HP/MP, and consume food buffs.
   - **Bounty Notice Board**: Accepts daily contracts to hunt mutated elite Colossus beasts for Gold Sovereigns and Prismatic Shards.

3. **The Alchemist’s Mortar**
   - **Apothecary**: Brews restorative draughts, clarity elixirs, stoneskin tinctures, and panacea vials from harvested botanical herbs.

4. **The Anchor Gate**
   - **Wayfarer Portal**: An ancient ward-ring capable of teleporting the Wayfarer directly to unlocked expedition sectors across Aethelgard.

## 3. Main Campaign Acts & Boss Encounters

### Act I: The Awakening Fog (Levels 1–10)
- **Zone**: *The Ashen Glade* (Mist-choked forest filled with mutated timber wolves and desperate scavengers).
- **Gear Bracket**: Tier 1–2 (*Threadbare Tunic*, *Boiled Leather*, *Rusted Shiv*, *Dull Training Blade*, *Birch Shortbow*, *Gnarled Oak Branch*).
- **Campaign Objective**: Cleanse the corrupted spring poisoning Haven’s central water supply.
- **Anchor Boss**: **Root-Hulk Malphas**
  - *Lore*: A hollow ironwood ancient animated by a leaking Prismatic Shard.
  - *Climax*: Slaying Malphas stabilizes the first Anchor and reveals deliberate sabotage behind the seal failures.

### Act II: The Weeping Tombs (Levels 10–20)
- **Zone**: *The Sunken Catacombs* (Flooded crypts, skeleton sentries, poisonous marsh leeches).
- **Gear Bracket**: Tier 3–4 (*Chainmail*, *Scholar's Silk*, *Serrated Dirk*, *Broadsword*, *Recurve Yew*, *Chipped Crystal Rod*).
- **Campaign Objective**: Track the rogue Ash-Sworn cult into the royal necropolis beneath Haven’s foundations.
- **Anchor Boss**: **High Priestess Valthea**
  - *Lore*: Fanatical leader of the Ash-Sworn who seeks to accelerate Titan awakening.
  - *Climax*: Confronted on the Sunken Dais, Valthea shatters the second Anchor as she falls, sending seismic shockwaves across the shelf.

### Act III: The Cinder Wastes (Levels 20–30)
- **Zone**: *Scorch-Rock Crags* (Sulfuric vents, volcanic drakes, rogue dwarven reavers).
- **Gear Bracket**: Tier 5–6 (*Reinforced Brigandine*, *Steel Cuirass*, *Tempered Falchion*, *Heavy Ironwood Longbow*, *Glacial Spire Staff*).
- **Campaign Objective**: Secure the high mountain passes to reach the Ancient Forge—the only hearth capable of tempering steel against Titan hide.
- **Anchor Boss**: **Ignis the Pyre Wyrm**
  - *Lore*: A massive dragon nesting over the geothermal heart of the Great Anvil.
  - *Climax*: Slay Ignis to reclaim the Great Anvil and forge weapons infused with dragon-scale temper.

### Act IV: The Abyss Unveiled (Levels 30–40)
- **Zone**: *The Sunless Depths* (Subterranean bioluminescent chasms, aberrant horrors, void heralds).
- **Gear Bracket**: Tier 7–8 (*Runed Spellthread*, *Shadow-Stalker Gambeson*, *Flame-Forged Longsword*, *Dragon-Sinew Warbow*, *Sylvan Living-Wood Cane*).
- **Campaign Objective**: Descend into the planetary mantle to reactivate the Master Seal before Gorgoroth fully awakens.
- **Anchor Boss**: **Void-Gazer Xar'koth**
  - *Lore*: An astral aberration born from the slumbering Titan's subconscious mind that feeds on mortal sanity.
  - *Climax*: Banish Xar'koth from the rift to stabilize the Master Seal's core chamber.

### Act V: The Shattered Ascent (Levels 40–50)
- **Zone**: *The Broken Crown* (Floating obsidian spires suspended in mid-air by raw, surging Aether).
- **Gear Bracket**: Tier 9–10 (*Dragonhide Hauberk*, *Abyssal Dreadplate*, *Mithril Greatsword*, *Sun-Shatter Blade*, *Void-String Phoenix Bow*, *Eclipse Void-Staff*).
- **World Titan Raid**: **Gorgoroth, the Earth-Breaker**
  - *Lore*: The Prime Titan whose slumbering pulse shatters mountains.
  - *Climax*: Multi-phase boss fight requiring the Wayfarer to target structural limbs, sunder heavy armor plates with elemental weaknesses, and brace against arena-shaking tremors.

## 4. Side Quests & World Encounters

### 1. The Blind Cartographer
- **Unlock**: Uncovered by finding a cracked compass in Act I (Ashen Glade).
- **Task**: Kindle beacon fires at high outlooks across all 5 zones.
- **Reward**: *Permanent Map Vision* (-15% ambush chance, +10% chest spawn rate).

### 2. Blood of the Forge
- **Unlock**: Unlocked by speaking with Torvald the Smith after acquiring Tier 3 gear.
- **Task**: Hunt 3 elite beasts: *Brine-Tusk Boar*, *Obsidian Crab*, and *Chitin Stalker*.
- **Reward**: *Masterwork Sockets* (allows locking 1 chosen affix during Prismatic Enchanter rerolls).

### 3. The Deserting Herald
- **World Event**: Encounter a wounded Ash-Sworn cult defector carrying ancient relics in the field.
- **Moral Choice**:
  - **Execute for Bounty**: +5 Silver Shillings and Haven Reputation.
  - **Heal Him**: Unlocks the Black Market Vendor in Haven's Rest.
  - **Rob Him**: Gains exotic relics (+10% Crit Chance) but incurs the *Curse of the Fallen* (-50 Max HP).

### 4. Colossus Bounties
- **Unlock**: Unlocked at the Rusty Goblet Notice Board at Level 15+.
- **Task**: Daily hunting contracts targeting elite monsters with randomized combat affixes (*Vampiric*, *Thorns*, *Juggernaut*).
- **Reward**: Prismatic Shards, Gold Sovereigns, and Ancient Chest Keys.

## 5. Itemization & Equipment Taxonomy

### Currency Tiers (100:1 Metal Ratio)
- **Copper Coins (CC)**: Base coin for street snacks, arrows, and low-tier draughts.
- **Silver Shillings (SS)**: $1\text{ SS} = 100\text{ CC}$. Used for mid-tier gear, inn stays, and standard upgrades.
- **Gold Sovereigns (GS)**: $1\text{ GS} = 100\text{ SS} = 10,000\text{ CC}$. Used for high-tier artifacts and exotic weapons.
- **Prismatic Shards (PS)**: Rare dungeon salvage used exclusively for rerolling randomized affixes at the Enchanter.

## 6. Narrative Tone & Dialogue Guidelines
- **Atmosphere**: Dark fantasy, high-tactility, melancholic yet heroic.
- **Style**: Evocative monospace terminal prose with rich sensory descriptions (sulfur smell, sound of grinding stone, hum of raw Aether).
- **UI Terminal Formatting**: High-contrast slate background with serif headers (`Cinzel`), gold/amber highlights (`Gold Sovereigns`), and emerald/crimson combat logs.
